## 3. Key Talking Points for Judges

1. **Why is this credible for enterprise healthcare?**
   - It respects clinical reality: it doesn't accuse doctors of fraud; it arms certified fraud examiners with defensible evidence, statistical baselines, and peer-group stratification.
2. **Why is the Fraud Genome transformative?**
   - Rather than reducing a provider to a single flat number, the 10-dimension genome creates an intuitive behavioral profile that reveals *how* and *where* the abuse is taking place.
3. **How does ClaimShield Nexus safeguard Responsible AI?**
   - Hard policy guardrails prevent automated claim denials, enforce standardized objective terminology, and mandate human sign-off for any operational sanction.
